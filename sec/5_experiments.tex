\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\paragraph{Protocol.}
We test every session in which a material is touched with a different unit or gel than in training. Three folds hold out campaign D, campaign C, and campaigns A and B together, and each trains on the remaining campaigns (\cref{tab:folds}). This gives 55 test sessions, 47 in which both the unit and the gel differ from training and 8 in which only the gel does. In every fold the test hardware also appears in the training set, tied to other materials. The low-pass width and the detector's constants were set on the fold that holds out D, which is also one of the three test folds. For the fold that holds out A and B, which trains on later campaigns and tests on earlier ones, we pre-registered three directional predictions for an earlier CNN version of the correction before training any model on it, and all three held (\supp{G}). The results below were run afterwards. We report episode accuracy averaged over the 55 sessions, with 95\pct\ bootstrap intervals over sessions, paired for differences between methods, and the share of test episodes in the most predicted class, which exposes a collapse.

\paragraph{Training and the stream.}
Unless stated otherwise, every method uses an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50~\cite{he2016resnet}, fine-tuned at the native \SI{11.3}{\micro\metre}/px on 192-px crops along the contact, which we call tube crops, with flips, quarter turns and photometric jitter, for 6{,}000 AdamW steps at batch size 64 (\supp{A}). We call this source-only training. An episode is classified by the mean log-probability of 24 crops, from three positions along the contact and eight frames of the stroke. Every adaptation method receives the same unlabeled stream: all contacts recorded on the test hardware in the test campaign. The stream therefore contains the test sessions, and row~i of \cref{tab:ablation} scores sessions it never contained. All methods are given the true change dates, so the correction and the test-time baselines reset together (\cref{sec:trackerexp} uses the detector's alarms).

\subsection{What each part contributes}
\label{sec:ablation}

\Cref{tab:ablation} adds the parts one at a time. Source-only, the ResNet-50 scores \XpRecipeAcc\pct, with \XpRecipeTop\pct\ of its test episodes in one class (a). The correction alone gains about twelve points without any training (b). Alignment alone gains more (c), and combining them is better than either alone (d). Subtracting the whole estimate from the full method's test images, fine pattern included, lowers its accuracy by about forty points, far below source-only training, consistent with the object residue of \cref{eq:residue}. When the training crops are corrected in the same way, the full estimate does no harm (\supp{N}). For the full method, narrower low-passes ($\sigma$ of 8 or 16\,px) lose ten to twenty points and wider ones (66 or 132\,px) up to two. For the correction alone, 16, 66 and 132\,px each lose six to nine. The width was set on the fold that holds out D, whose 20 sessions are part of this sweep. With DANN~\cite{ganin2016dann} in place of CDAN+E the method loses less than a point (e). Every part helps on the two folds that cross units, and none on the fold that holds out C, with four other-gel sessions and one of wood. The correction alone gains 22 points on the fold that holds out D, where its width was set, and nine on the fold that holds out A and B.

A confidence objective~\cite{zhang2022memo,liang2020shot} gains a few points with an interval that includes zero on the average of several contacts (f) and costs about five on every contact (g, \supp{D}). Correcting the training images costs about two points (h).

Row d is transductive, because the stream already contains the test contacts. We therefore also align with the first half of each configuration's test sessions and score the second half (i). About three quarters of the alignment gain remains overall, most of it in the fold that holds out A and B, where the later sessions repeat the materials of the earlier ones. On the \XpLateOtherN\ later sessions of the other folds, whose materials the stream never contained, alignment adds \XdLateOtherFull\ points with the full stream and \XdLateOtherEarly\ with the first half.

\subsection{Comparison with published methods}
\label{sec:baselines}

Every published method in \cref{tab:baselines} runs with its paper's default settings, except DANN (\supp{D}), on the same encoder, training budget and stream. SDAT and MIC keep the better of two trade-off weights, chosen on the first seed, which favors them over our fixed weight (\supp{D}).

\paragraph{Test time.}
Four of the five test-time methods collapse on the robot's stream, to about \XpTtaBnAcc\pct. Per-batch BatchNorm, Tent, SAR and DeYO estimate normalization statistics from the current batch, which holds a single material here, so the statistics normalize it away. RoTTA, designed for correlated streams, is the one that works, gaining \XdRottaVsSrc\ points over the unadapted model. Image correction performs comparably without batch statistics or gradient updates, raising accuracy from \XpSrcAcc\ to \XpSrcReadAcc\pct, \XdSrcReadVsRotta\ points above RoTTA with an interval that includes zero (\XdSrcReadVsRottaLo\ to \XdSrcReadVsRottaHi). RoTTA is ahead only when A and B are held out (\XpTtaRottaFoldAB\ against \XpReadFoldAB\pct).

\paragraph{Retraining.}
Every retraining method improves on source-only training, and the correction improves each of them further, by three to ten points with intervals that exclude zero. The gain comes from the 47 sessions of the other unit and gel. On the eight sessions of the other gel the correction lowers every method (\cref{sec:discussion}). With the correction, DANN, SDAT, MIC and CDAN+E end within three points of each other. Our method, CDAN+E with the correction, is highest at \XpFullAcc\pct, with a lead inside the interval (\XdFullVsDannRead\ points above corrected DANN over five seeds, \XdFullVsDannReadLo\ to \XdFullVsDannReadHi). FDA~\cite{fda2020} gains half as much as the correction alone (\supp{D}).

\subsection{Other encoders and views}
\label{sec:encoders}

The gains hold for three other encoders on tube crops, a CNN trained from scratch, ConvNeXt-T and DINOv2-B~\cite{oquab2024dinov2} (\supp{X}). The correction raises each by ten to twenty points, and alignment adds to it. On crops placed anywhere in the frame, the correction alone does not help ResNet-50 or ConvNeXt-T. On top of alignment it adds \XdDinoFrameReadVsCdan\ points for DINOv2-B but lowers ResNet-50 by nine.

\subsection{Public datasets}
\label{sec:second}

In the controlled setting of \cref{sec:shortcut} with one class tied to the test Mini, a frozen DINOv2 with an MLP head recovers from \CfSitrRawOneCross\ to \CfSitrStreamOneCross\pct\ on the other classes when the test Mini's mean image is subtracted, an oracle (\cref{fig:confound}), and to \CfTrkSitrOneCross\pct\ with a causal variant of our estimate that sees the Mini's objects one after another. On Real Tactile MNIST this variant raises the same measure from \CfTmdRawOneCross\ to \CfTrkTmOneCross\pct, against \CfTmdRawZeroCross\pct\ without the tie (\supp{J}). Unlike \cref{eq:correction}, these runs subtract the full band and correct the training images as well. SITR's Minis also differ in field of view and in contrast by a factor of \SmContrastRatio\ (\supp{K}), which no per-pixel correction removes, and a CNN trained from scratch stays at about \SmStreamCross\pct. With four classes tied to the test Mini and its images as the stream, CDAN+E raises such a CNN from \XsJit\ to \XsCdan\pct\ on the other 16 classes (\supp{L}), and half as much when the stream holds 5 of the 20 classes.

\subsection{The estimate in practice}
\label{sec:practice}
\label{sec:trackerexp}

In an earlier protocol, which subtracts the full estimate and also corrects the training crops (\supp{N} and Q), a CNN tested on campaign D scores \CvsSupObjOne\pct\ with an estimate built from one other object, \CvsSupObjTwo\pct\ with two and \CvsSupObjNineteen\pct\ with nineteen. The session's own bare-gel frame, the reference that background subtraction needs, gives \SrcSupBlankAcc\pct, and no correction \SrcSupNoneAcc\pct. With photometric jitter in training these become \CvsAugObjNineteen, \SrcAugBlankAcc\ and \SrcAugNoneAcc\pct\ for nineteen objects, the bare-gel frame and no correction. Subtracting the previous session's state lowers the linear probe of \cref{sec:shortcut} to \ProbeIjepaPrevUnit\ to \ProbeDinovPrevUnit\pct, near chance (50\pct, \supp{R}). Restarting the estimate at the detector's alarms instead of the true dates changes the full method's accuracy by \XdFullVarDetVsLp\ points (\XdFullVarDetVsLpLo\ to \XdFullVarDetVsLpHi, \supp{U}).

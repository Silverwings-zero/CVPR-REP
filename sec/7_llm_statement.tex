\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Generative AI tools were used in this work. Claude (Anthropic; Claude Code) was used, under the authors' direction, to implement experiment and analysis scripts, run and log experiments, generate figures from the result files, and draft and edit the text of all sections of this paper. Every experimental design decision, the framing and claims, and the interpretation of results were made by the authors; every number in the paper is generated from logged results by a script, and the authors verified the results and the text. No AI tool was used to fabricate data, results or citations.

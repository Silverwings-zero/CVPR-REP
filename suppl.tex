\section{Data and protocol details}
\paragraph{Recording.} GelSight Mini (native 1640$\times$1232, \SI{11.3}{\micro\metre}/px, \SI{18.8}{fps}) on one finger of a Franka parallel gripper, ATI Nano17 on the other (host-timestamped at 130--190\,Hz; normal force valid in all campaigns). Episodes: 20 materials; commanded normal force 0.9--5\,N; stroke 10--100\,mm/s. Campaigns: A June 15--22 (15 sessions, 570 episodes), B July 6--8 (15, 450), C July 9--13 (5, 340), D September 22 -- October 15 (20, 1{,}351); every session holds one material. Hardware: unit 1 with gel 1 from June 15 to July 9 (state S1); unit 2 with gel 2 from July 10 to October 9, 18:42 (S2); unit 2 with gel 3 from the cork session of October 9, 19:37 (S3). The laboratory log recorded four gel replacements and a change of unit ``between campaigns''; the images record the two changes above (fine-pattern blocks, marker layouts and illumination fields all agree), which the operators confirmed. Campaign D also recorded 620 bare-gel frames in 44 sessions (a start blank and lubricant pre/post pairs) and, in seven sessions, a 3-s bare-gel lead-in before every grasp.
\paragraph{Crops and frames.} Material models read 12 native 192-px crops per episode (4 tube anchors $\times$ 3 fixed positions inside the stroke window), markers inpainted per frame; the eight-frame variant sums the 8 consecutive frames of each tube exactly (uint16). Force and contact models read whole native frames: four per episode at the 10/40/70/95\,\% quantiles of the episode's force, plus lead-in, pre-contact and onset frames (force crossing 0.05/0.2/0.5\,N) where a lead-in exists.
\paragraph{State fields.} Per session, the position-wise sum and count of its contact crops in frame coordinates (Bayesian shrinkage with weight 2 toward the all-session mean at low coverage); a crop's own episode is left out when its own session is the source. Previous-session calibration uses the full field of the session immediately preceding in wall-clock order. The reference field $G$ is the mean over training sessions.
\paragraph{Signature measurement.} For each sampled contact crop the 190--370\,\si{\micro\metre} band (difference of 17- and 33-px box means of the grey image) is correlated with the same band of a partner session's state field over pixels covered by at least three of that field's crops and at least 60\,px from every printed marker of the session (markers detected on the session's first raw frame; inpainting radius $\le$59\,px, band support 16\,px). With the marker surroundings included the same-hardware medians are 0.05 higher and the cross-hardware floor rises from \HwFarOtherUnit\ to \HwOtherUnit, which is inpainting residue. Partner categories by the hardware timeline: own session (episode left out), other object same day, same unit and gel on another day within a month, same unit and gel three months apart (July 10--13 against September 28 -- October 9), the other unit, the other gel of the same unit.

\paragraph{The tracker.} Window $W=30$ episodes, $k=2$ pseudo-counts toward $G$. Change statistics per episode: median over its crops of the fine-band correlation with the window mean (pixels with window count $\ge 3$, $\ge 60$\,px from markers) and the mean absolute difference of the Gaussian-smoothed ($\sigma=33$\,px) grey images. Thresholds were searched on a grid (fine $\tau_f\in\{p_5, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3\}$, illumination $\tau_i\in\{p_{95}, p_{99}, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5\}$ grey levels, persistence $m\in\{1,2,3,5,8\}$, percentiles from the first hardware state's \num{1086} episodes): \TrkSettingsGood\ of \TrkSettingsTotal\ settings detect both changes with no false alarm; the operating point ($\tau_f=\TrkTauF$, $\tau_i=\TrkTauI$, $m=\TrkM$) has latencies \TrkUnitLatency\ and \TrkGelLatency\ episodes, the pure-percentile setting ($\tau_f=\TrkPctTauF$, $\tau_i=p_{95}=\TrkIllumPninetyfive$, $m=\TrkPctM$) \TrkPctUnitLatency\ and \TrkPctGelLatency. After an alarm detection is held for $W$ episodes while the window turns over. On Real Tactile MNIST (320$\times$240 SDK frames, $\sim$58\,\si{\micro\metre}/px): band 3--7\,px, $\sigma=6$\,px, marker radius 11\,px, $W=60$ touches, thresholds from the first gel's first three days; \TmTrkGood\ of \TmTrkTotal\ settings detect the gel swap with no false alarm, the best within \TmTrkLatency\ touches.

\paragraph{Photometric readings tested and rejected.} Besides subtraction ($x-\mu_F+\mu_G$): the ratio $x\,\mu_G/\mu_F$; per-pixel, per-channel location and scale ($(x-\mu_F)\,s_G/s_F+\mu_G$, $s$ the low-pass standard deviation of a session's contacts); and a per-pixel $3\times3$ colour-response alignment $C_G^{1/2}C_F^{-1/2}(x-\mu_F)+\mu_G$ from the low-pass colour covariances of the contacts. On the fold that trains on A+B+C and tests on D (plain CNN\,/\,jitter CNN, previous-session source): subtraction 51.7\,/\,55.8, ratio 51.8\,/\,55.4, location and scale 48.0\,/\,46.5, colour alignment 47.1\,/\,46.2; with the campaign's contacts as the source: 54.9\,/\,53.0, 56.1\,/\,51.0, 39.3\,/\,31.4, 47.7\,/\,45.7. The richer models do not help, so the gap that remains after subtraction is not a per-pixel photometric difference.

\paragraph{Public datasets.} SITR classification set: 20 objects on seven sensors (sensors 0, 4, 5, 6 are GelSight Minis), 800 training and 200 test images per object and sensor at 224\,px as released; we use 200 training images per class from the training sensor and 100 test images per class from every sensor, a frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 (mean of patch tokens), MLP heads as in the main text, and the hardware probe is a logistic regression on the test features with class-stratified five-fold cross-validation. FEATS: samples are 240$\times$320 images with FEA force maps; the training set is dominated by one sensor, the two held-out splits are another sensor with its old and with a new gel; sensor identity of a sample is taken from the nearest no-contact template by the image periphery (the sensor is unambiguous, the gel within a sensor is not), so the background reading uses the sensor-level mean of the no-contact frames; the regressor is our encoder stem on the full 240$\times$320 image with a two-layer head on $\log(1+|f_z|)$, eight epochs. Real Tactile MNIST: as in the main text.

\paragraph{Heads and statistics.} MLP heads (256 hidden, dropout 0.2, 40 epochs, AdamW) on standardised frozen embeddings, three seeds; per-episode pooled logits; macro accuracy, macro-F1 and the top-class share. Confidence intervals: paired bootstrap over the 20 materials (4{,}000 resamples).

\section{Force and contact details}
Marker grids are detected on the bare gel at native resolution (63--64 dots, the Mini's 7$\times$9 grid), matched to each contact frame's dots within 0.6 grid spacings, and summarised by the affine fit of the displacement field (divergence, mean displacement, net shear). A per-dot learned head (MLP on the 126-d displacement field) gives no gain over the affine summary and breaks across the gel swap (the new gel's dot order differs). Stale \emph{unloaded} references for appearance-based force: $R^2$ 0.09 (fresh) vs $-$1.9 (previous session) vs $-$13 (older); for marker geometry the force ranking survives any same-gel reference (Spearman 0.65--0.67). Contact: heads trained on the seven lead-in sessions (lead-in and pre-contact frames = no contact; onset and contact frames = contact), tested on the other 13 sessions' contact frames and 620 bare-gel frames; blank-differencing threshold fitted on the lead-in sessions.

\section{Measurement details}
Band-pass: box filters of 3 and 33 px (34--370\,\si{\micro\metre}); marker blobs of each session's own field masked (radius 30 px). Reliability of a band = correlation of two disjoint $k$-frame means of the same crop (120 episodes). Codec-row share: power at the 8/16-row harmonics of the marker-free session field over its total band power (median 3.4\pct\ vs 4.7\pct\ of the spectrum). Real Tactile MNIST: 320$\times$240 stills as delivered; bands 3--7 and 7--15 px; one run's touches at random cell positions.
